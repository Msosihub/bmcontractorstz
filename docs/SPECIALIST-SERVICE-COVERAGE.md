## Recommended final navigation and hierarchy

Keep Security & Safety first in desktop navigation, opening direct links to CCTV installation, electric fencing and gate automation, plus the full Security division. Show those three direct links first in the mobile menu. Follow with All services, Equipment, Projects and Contact, bilingual language controls and one primary enquiry action. The homepage retains its early security content and approved architectural composition.

The All services page now groups the routes as follows:

- **Security, Safety & Automation**: cctv, electric-fence, gate-motors, access-control, fire-alarm-systems, video-intercom.

- **Electrical Engineering & Contracting**: electrical-contracting.

- **Power Generation, Solar & Backup**: generators, solar-panel-systems, power-backup, solar-street-lights.

- **ICT, Networking & Business Telephony**: networking, telephone-systems.

- **HVAC, Equipment Supply & Technical Support**: air-conditioners, maintenance-support, institutional-procurement.

Institutional procurement is listed with equipment supply for navigation, but serves all divisions. This is information architecture, not a claim that these are separately registered organizational departments.

- Video intercom: equipment/interfaces, service checks and existing-gate compatibility FAQ.
- Air conditioning: room/load factors, equipment/drainage, maintenance and capacity-selection FAQ.
- Solar outdoor lighting: system components, battery/mounting maintenance and operating-duration FAQ.
- Maintenance: fault/parts scope, records, service planning and useful diagnostic enquiry information.
- Generators: ATS is now immediately visible in the service title; existing load, changeover, installation and service-parts content is retained.
- All specialties: grouped directory, relevant equipment searches and related-service links; an early institutional/BOQ link on core service pages.

## Genuinely missing pages / route decisions

No new dedicated route is justified yet: the confirmed specialties fit the sixteen existing routes. Separate attendance, intrusion alarm or ATS child pages may later be useful for distinct search intent, but should require verified delivered configurations, separate assessment/testing and maintenance scope, useful original FAQs, and a clear distinction from the parent. Creating them now would duplicate existing content.

Broader HVAC categories (such as ventilation, chillers or industrial heating), payroll/ERP/PMS integrations, certified inspection/sign-off, emergency monitoring/guard response and manufacturer-authorized status are not inferred from the approved scope. Any such page requires additional owner evidence and distinct content.
