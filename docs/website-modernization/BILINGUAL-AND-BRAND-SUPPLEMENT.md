# Mandatory bilingual experience and official identity
Owner instruction, 16 October 2026. Supplements FINAL-EXECUTION-INSTRUCTION.md.

English (en) and Kiswahili (sw) are permanent public experiences. English is the default unless real usage/SEO evidence justifies otherwise. Neither language is optional or scheduled for removal.

Maintain coverage across homepage, services/security/electrical/power/generators/procurement, catalogue/details, projects, forms and errors/success, navigation/footer and metadata. Keep product/model names accurate; identify missing translations rather than fabricate them. New corporate content remains gated by verified capability.

Phase 1A preserves current paths and ?lang=sw URLs; no major migration. Use language-specific self-canonicals, reciprocal hreflang where content is translated, correct HTML lang, crawlable links and localized sitemap entries. No automatic locale redirects or cookie/JS-only discovery. Incomplete SW product/package content stays accessible and is visibly marked; temporary noindex/hreflang omission is documented for review until translations are complete, not a language-removal policy.

Phase 1B uses the supplied official logo assets and reviewed design. Preserve proportions, artwork, original colors and originals. No generated replacement logo. Optimize delivery formats/sizes without altering artwork; retain rollback assets. Review translation quality and language navigation.

Later URL migration requires its own baseline, redirect/hreflang plan and monitoring. No production deployment without preview evidence and explicit approval.
