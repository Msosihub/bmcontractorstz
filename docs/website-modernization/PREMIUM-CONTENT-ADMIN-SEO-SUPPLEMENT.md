# BM Contractors — Premium Page Content, Admin UX & SEO Enrichment

This instruction supplements the existing BM Contractors website modernization project.

Continue using the approved luxury corporate visual direction, original BM logo, generated illustrative imagery and bilingual English/Kiswahili support.

Our objective is to make every page not only visually beautiful, but informative, credible, useful and commercially effective.

## PART A — Public website content enrichment

As each service page is redesigned, improve the depth and quality of its content.

The content should help visitors understand what BM offers, identify appropriate solutions and confidently request services or quotations.

For appropriate service pages, include:

1. A meaningful introduction explaining the service.
2. Common applications and customer requirements.
3. Specific equipment, systems or technologies.
4. Types of installations and project scope.
5. A clear explanation of the typical assessment and installation process.
6. Relevant verified technical specifications or considerations.
7. Maintenance and after-sales support information.
8. Related services and products.
9. Frequently asked customer questions.
10. Genuine approved project examples where available.
11. Clear contact, site survey or RFQ actions.

Do not create ten repetitive sections on every page. Select the sections that genuinely serve each service.

### Priority services

- CCTV and surveillance.
- Electric fencing.
- Automatic gate systems.
- Access control and attendance.
- Intruder and fire alarm systems.
- Electrical engineering and installation.
- Generator systems and ATS/changeover.
- Solar, UPS and backup power.
- Networking, structured cabling and Wi-Fi.
- Office and hotel telephone systems.
- HVAC.
- Technical equipment supply.
- Maintenance and support.
- Institutional procurement.

Preserve existing service URLs, especially CCTV, electric fence and gate motors.

## PART B — SEO content quality

Ensure every indexable public page has:

- An accurate, unique title and description.
- An appropriate main heading.
- Meaningful subordinate headings.
- Original, useful service or product information.
- Descriptive internal links.
- Correct canonical and language-alternate metadata.
- Relevant local and national service information.
- Appropriate structured data.
- Accurate image alt text.
- Relevant customer FAQs where useful.

Preserve the two-language architecture.

Use natural professional English and Tanzanian Kiswahili.

Do not create keyword-stuffed paragraphs, repetitive geographic doorway pages or invented claims about branches.

Use genuine customer questions and search intent to guide content creation.

Do not invent company qualifications, technical specifications, equipment pricing or project history.

## PART C — Visual storytelling

Use premium generated illustrative imagery for service explanations where approved real project photos are not available.

Create coordinated image treatments for:
- Electrical engineering.
- CCTV surveillance.
- Electric fence installations.
- Gate automation.
- Generator and backup power systems.
- Networking infrastructure.
- Telephone systems.
- Institutional applications.

Optimize images for web performance.

Do not use AI illustrations as documentary proof of BM projects.

Maintain the classic, high-end graphite, ivory and refined architectural visual direction.

## PART D — Administration dashboard improvements

Inspect the existing BM Admin Dashboard and product management interface.

The objective is a highly usable, professional internal workspace.

Improve progressively:

### Navigation
- Compact, responsive admin navigation.
- Clear active section.
- Collapsible mobile menu.
- Consistent headings and spacing.
- Accessible controls.

### Product management
- Proper product name and model fields.
- Brand and category management.
- Clear description fields.
- Structured specifications where appropriate.
- Retail and wholesale pricing readiness.
- Product publication states.
- Appropriate availability status.
- Image preview and future secure upload workflow.
- More useful product list, search and filters.
- Clear edit, publish and unpublish actions.
- Visible confirmation of successful operations.

### Data quality
- Prevent unintended category duplication.
- Validate product records.
- Distinguish unpublished test records from genuine products.
- Keep actual stock, prices and specifications authoritative.
- Protect internal cost and margin information.

### Dashboard usability
- Meaningful summaries.
- Helpful empty states.
- Concise field guidance.
- Clear validation messages.
- Useful status explanations.
- Consistent loading, completion and error states.

Do not build an entirely separate admin application.

Reuse the existing authentication, database models and protected Server Actions.

Do not add product image upload endpoints until the associated storage, file validation, access control and cleanup requirements are properly designed.

## PART E — Separate public SEO from admin UX

Only public marketing, service, product and applicable project pages should be optimized for search discovery.

The administration area must remain protected and non-indexable.

Do not expose private products, internal prices, customer records, unpublished project information or administrative data to search engines.

## PART F — Work in controlled phases

First, preserve and complete the current Phase 1A safety work and release-readiness requirements.

Then establish the approved luxury design system.

Develop one polished, content-rich service page as the reusable quality standard, preferably CCTV.

After reviewing that result, apply the same content and visual quality to related services without creating repetitive templates.

Improve the administration interface in a separate reviewable set of changes.

Do not introduce inventory, accounting, payments or unrelated systems merely to support UI improvements.

## PART G — Review requirements

For every substantially redesigned page, verify:

- Visual quality on desktop and mobile.
- Useful information depth.
- English and Kiswahili quality.
- Functioning enquiry actions.
- Accurate technical claims.
- SEO metadata.
- Accessibility.
- Loading performance.
- No accidental publication of test data.
- No regression in existing functionality.

Provide comparison screenshots and a brief explanation of substantive improvements.

## FINAL STANDARD

BM Contractors should present itself as a credible, sophisticated national engineering, security and technology company.

Its website must be beautiful enough to impress, informative enough to answer real questions, and clear enough to produce qualified customer enquiries.

Its administration interface must make staff more efficient without sacrificing security or data quality.

Prioritize substance, clarity, professionalism and business outcomes over adding unnecessary features or increasing word count.

Do not deploy to production without explicit approval.
