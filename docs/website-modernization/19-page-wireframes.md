# Reviewable page concepts
Open phase-1b-design-proposal.html and use its eight concept tabs and English/Kiswahili buttons. It is a review artifact with no backend or live submission.

1. Homepage: official identity, visible bilingual selector, security-led hero, survey/project CTAs, six divisions, organizational procurement entry and simple process.
2. Service detail: preserve established service path and intent; overview, verified scope, equipment/process, FAQ/evidence and survey CTA.
3. Electrical: dedicated approved scope, qualifications boundary, materials supply and institutional enquiry. Credential/capacity placeholders are not public claims.
4. Institutional procurement: capabilities, approved profile/renewable credentials, relevant evidence and basic RFQ fields. Attachments remain deferred.
5. Catalogue: search, category filters and paginated accurate cards, request-price/availability states and quantity enquiry.
6. Product: real name/model, approved image/datasheet/specifications, pricing mode, quantity and compatible accessories. No guessed specification or stock.
7. Projects: real consented evidence and completion status; examples/quotes/tenders never presented as completed work.
8. Mobile navigation: compact official shield/name, visible languages, clear service/product/project/contact links and survey CTA.

Corporate/generator claims remain unverified until the evidence register is completed. Generator content belongs within Power Generation, Solar & Backup. The prototype uses explicit photograph/product placeholders, not fabricated case studies or offers.

Review criteria: identity accuracy, security prominence, natural Kiswahili, readable mobile hierarchy, institution path and simplicity. Phase 1B implementation requires approval.
