Generators stay Division C. Join content phases only when sourcing/competence evidence is ready.
Do not build ERP, custom accounting, brokers, microservices, marketplaces, subscriptions or mobile apps without demonstrated demand.
Dependencies: management review, deployment owner, safe preview/backups and real evidence.
Production deploy/migrations/customer messages remain controlled separately. No promised revenue, rankings, schedule or effort estimate beyond relative prioritization.
