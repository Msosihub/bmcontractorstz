## 2. Door access control

Cover practical installations such as:

- Single-door fingerprint or PIN access.
- RFID/card access.
- Facial-recognition access.
- Multi-door controller-based systems.
- Office and institutional access.
- Restricted rooms and staff areas.
- Apartment and commercial entrances.
- Visitor and contractor access.

Research appropriate product families including:

- F18 / F18 Pro and current comparable terminals.
- SenseFace and SpeedFace families.
- InBio160/260/460 and InBio Pro Plus controllers.
- C2/C3 controller families.
- KR-series readers and compatible readers.
- ZKTeco door-access accessories.

BM also works with PIN-only access and controller-based configurations. Clearly explain when a standalone device is sufficient and when a separate controller is appropriate.

Do not claim universal device/software compatibility.

### Explain the complete system

Create an elegant original technical illustration showing:

Reader or biometric terminal → access controller → electric lock → door sensor / exit button → software / network.

Include power supply and appropriate backup arrangements.

Explain the roles of electromagnetic locks, electric strikes, exit buttons, emergency release, readers, cards and controllers.

Address safe emergency egress, fire-alarm integration where relevant and site-specific requirements.

The diagram should be technically meaningful, visually beautiful, responsive and accessible.

**ZKBio Time Cloud / BioTime Cloud**

**ZKBio CVAccess**

An access-control-oriented platform for suitable small and medium installations, with its verified supported modules.

**ZKBio CVSecurity**

A more comprehensive physical-security management platform where appropriate for larger or advanced integrated systems.

**Hotel lock management software**

- Hardware compatibility.
