# Fire Safety — source and commercial boundaries
Reviewed 26 November 2026. Internal implementation evidence; not a building compliance determination.

## Primary legal sources inspected
- [Fire and Rescue Force Act, Cap.427 R.E.2023](https://oagmis.oag.go.tz/portal/acts/revised/527/download): Mainland application; building/escape approval and fire-protection responsibilities. Relevant sections 2, 23, 25, 36. No BM qualification inferred.
- [GN533/2015, Fire Precautions in Buildings](https://polis.parliament.go.tz/uploads/bills/1459254298-GN%20533%20THE%20FIRE%20AND%20RESCUE%20FORCE%20%28FIRE%20PRECAUTIONS%20IN%20BUILDINGS%29%20REGULATIONS_2015.pdf): full 243-page official PDF downloaded, text extracted; printed pages 168–170 rendered and reviewed. Reg.244(5) has occupancy-specific 100/200/400 m² provisions and agent/capacity requirements. Its listed 9kg dry-chemical and 5kg CO₂ provisions are **not** a universal rule for every room or school. 6kg DCP remains a commercial assessment option, not a presumed compliant substitute. Rating, hazard, occupancy, approved positioning and authority directions matter.
- Reg.244(7): inspection on placement and approximately every 30 days (more often where needed); maintenance no more than one year apart, earlier where indicated; recharge after use/where indicated; service tags/records. Trained personnel and suitable tools/manuals are required; pressure testing has additional competence/equipment requirements. No DIY servicing instructions published.
- [OSHA Act Cap.297 R.E.2023](https://oagmis.oag.go.tz/portal/acts/revised/401/download), particularly s.50: workplace fire precautions, escape, staff familiarity and inspection obligations. These are separate from supplier marketing.
- [GN539R/2024 official record](https://oagmis.oag.go.tz/portal/legislation/3806) and its downloaded gazette: amends the **Third Schedule of the 2008 inspection/certificates/levy regulations**, moving the Fire Warden course classification between levy groups. It does not establish a new universal extinguisher size or replace GN533 technical design provisions. No fee quoted.
- [Official Government Fire and Rescue public notice](https://blog.maelezo.go.tz/post/taarifa-kwa-umma-62): describes registered fire dealers and the Force's supervisory role. Its regulation-number reference differs from the downloaded GN533 technical text; we rely on the actual gazette for Reg.244.
- Official OAG/FRF/OSHA searches for later changes were performed. They do **not** establish a legally certified, exhaustive consolidated rulebook through November 2026. The competent authority must confirm current site-specific requirements before work. Zanzibar requirements must be checked separately.

## Public claim boundaries
- Detection, notification and suppression have different functions. CCTV analytics, standalone and Wi-Fi alarms are not presumed substitutes for a required engineered alarm.
- Conventional zone circuits are drawn separately, with EOL supervision and a separate sounder circuit. Addressable loop is conceptual, not an installation drawing; exact topology, isolators, power and device limits follow the actual panel manual.
- Interfaces, specialist suppression, refill and hydrostatic/pressure testing are separately scoped against verified authorization. No claim BM issues government certificates, has a fire-dealer licence already verified, owns specialist test equipment, or automatically obtains approvals.
- Hospital phased evacuation is conditional on the applicable approved plan.
- BM installation/commissioning records are not statutory Fire Safety Certificates.
- No fixed strand/voltage/coverage/quantity or turnkey compliance promise; no invented price, stock, warranty, client project or manufacturer authorization.

## Owner confirmations still needed before regulated work is advertised unconditionally
1. Current BM fire-dealer registration and precise permitted activities.
2. Named competent design/installation/testing personnel and applicable credentials.
3. Authorization and delivery arrangement for extinguisher servicing, recharge and cylinder-pressure testing.
4. Specialist suppression scope/commissioning authorization and any subcontract arrangements.
5. Current supplier quotes, exact model revisions, regional approvals, ratings, accessories and lead times; particularly 6/9kg ABC and 5kg CO₂ units. NAFFCO photos are family references, not confirmed BM inventory or a sourcing promise.
6. Current authority interpretation for each actual premises; official inspection fees/procedures are not sold as BM certifications.

## Model choices
- Hikvision HF-C104: official brochure family HF-C104(8). Do not infer supplied variant's zone count from family notation. Exact regional manual and compatible devices required. Photograph sourced from the CERCASUL model page; technical authority remains the Hikvision brochure.
- ASENWARE AW-FP300: official page specifies one loop/up to324 addresses, 32-zone indication, 4.3-inch display and own protocol. Capacity remains subject to loading/manual/revision.
- AW-D301: photoelectric smoke, specifically matched to AW-FP300; routine dust/steam/oil mist/background smoke excluded by manufacturer guidance.
- AW-D302: addressable heat, A2 class, AW-FP300 family; no automatic replacement for sleeping-area smoke detection.
- AW-D305: addressable manual initiation, not notification or suppression.
- AW-D306: sounder/beacon, loop plus separate24V supply; IP21C, no outdoor/loop-only promise.
- AW-FP128 withheld: conflicting family/revision descriptions; no speculative SKU.
- Manufacturer approvals shown on original equipment photographs are not BM company qualifications or verified current local approval for every revision.
